Do you work with Nepali stores that use COD, eSewa or Khalti?

Yes. Nepali stores usually take a mix of cash on delivery, eSewa and Khalti, and that mix changes what converts. Checkout setup, delivery expectations and payment options are reviewed as part of the audit, because payment friction is one of the most common causes of abandoned carts in Nepal.

How long does it take to see results?

Paid campaigns can produce sales within the first few weeks once conversion tracking is correct. Ecommerce SEO usually takes three to six months before category and product pages move, because rankings build gradually. Email flows typically show results within the first month.
